      🖼️
      iPad → Photo Frame
      ~2 minute setup
      1
      Disable auto-lock. Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.
      2
      Max brightness. Swipe up for Control Center and drag to 100%.
      3
      Launch & prop. Open the Photo Frame app, plug in the charger, and prop the device up. Done.
      ✕
      💊
      Phone → Med Reminder
      ~3 minute setup
      1
      Place the phone somewhere visible — kitchen counter, bathroom shelf.
      2
      Open MediTrack Lite below and add your medications with their times.
      3
      Tap the circle each time you take a dose to track it.
      4
      Bookmark this page to open it directly every day. Data saves automatically.
      ✕
      🕐
      Any Device → Bedside Clock
      ~1 minute setup
      1
      Launch Clock Dashboard and tap Fullscreen Mode.
      2
      Disable auto-lock in settings so the screen stays on. Plug in the charger beside the bed.
      ✕
      📞
      Tablet → Emergency Hub
      ~2 minute setup
      1
      Place the tablet somewhere easily reachable — bedside, kitchen, living room.
      2
      Add key contacts — family, doctor, pharmacy. No login needed.
      3
      Keep it charged. Leave this page open. One tap to call anyone.
      ✕
      📍
      Old Tablet → Info Kiosk
      ~5 minute setup
      1
      Mount or prop the tablet on a wall or stand in a common area.
      2
      Open a dashboard site — try weather.com, your Google Calendar, or a family task board.
      3
      Enable Guided Access (iOS) or pin the app (Android) so it stays on that screen.
      4
      Plug it in. A perpetually-charged info hub costs nothing extra to run.
      ✕
      📷
      Old Phone → Security Cam
      ~10 minute setup
      1
      Download Alfred (free) on both the old phone and your main phone.
      2
      Sign into the same account on both devices. The old phone becomes the camera.
      3
      Position the old phone — facing the door, baby's crib, or any area you want to monitor.
      4
      Plug it in. A permanently-powered camera is more reliable than battery-only.
      5
      View the feed live from your main phone anywhere in the world.
      ✕
      📍
      Info Kiosk / Dashboard
      Turn an idle tablet into a smart home display
      1
      Mount or prop the tablet in a common area — kitchen, hallway, living room.
      2
      Open a browser dashboard — weather, Google Calendar, family task board, or news.
      3
      Lock it to that screen using Guided Access (iOS) or screen pinning (Android).
      4
      Plug in the charger. Zero extra hardware needed, zero e-waste.
      ✕
      📷
      Security / Baby Monitor
      A free camera from hardware you already own
      1
      Use the Alfred app (free) — install on the old phone and your current phone.
      2
      Old phone becomes the camera. Your current phone is the viewer.
      3
      Position, plug in, and you have a live security feed for free.
      ✕
      📚
      Dedicated E-reader
      One device, one purpose — pure reading
      1
      Install a lightweight reader — Moon+ Reader (Android) or Marvin (iOS) work on older OS versions.
      2
      Set display to warm/night mode for comfortable reading.
      3
      Airplane mode on = better battery, zero notifications. A distraction-free reading device.
      ✕
      🎵
      Dedicated Music Player
      Bring your old iPod back to life
      1
      Load your local music files via iTunes or direct transfer.
      2
      Use a lightweight streaming app — Spotify Lite and YouTube Music work on older OS.
      3
      Keep it in airplane mode when using local files for extended battery life.
      ✕
      🚨
      Emergency Contact Hub
      Old phone as a lifeline device
      1
      Keep an old phone charged and placed somewhere accessible — bedside table, kitchen.
      2
      Load it with the Emergency Hub app. Add family members, doctor, pharmacy.
      3
      One tap to call anyone. Works even if the SIM is active on a basic plan.
      ✕
      🎓
      Kids Learning Device
      Educational, focused, and distraction-free
      1
      Factory reset the old tablet for a clean start.
      2
      Set up parental controls — Screen Time (iOS) or Family Link (Android).
      3
      Install only educational apps — Khan Academy Kids, Duolingo, Scratch Jr.
      4
      Use Guided Access or screen pinning to keep kids in approved apps only.
      ✕
      🖼️
      Digital Photo Frame
      Repurpose any tablet or phone
      1
      Disable auto-lock in settings so the screen stays on permanently.
      2
      Open RebootOS Photo Frame app — a fullscreen rotating slideshow starts immediately.
      3
      Prop the device up, plug in the charger. A living photo frame with zero extra cost.
